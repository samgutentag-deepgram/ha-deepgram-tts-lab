has never run, which means the streaming path that just landed on `main` has never been
exercised the way every user will exercise it. The Voice PE is on order. Until it arrives, do not
write or shoot anything that claims Assist works.

Also unproven: whether Sonos `loadAudioClip` starts playing on first bytes or buffers the whole
clip. If it buffers, streaming through the Beam is inaudible and the first-frame number never
reaches a listener. That is a claim no piece may make yet.
